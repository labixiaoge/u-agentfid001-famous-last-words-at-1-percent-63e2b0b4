## About

An entry for the make.fun bounty
[u-agentfid001-famous-last-words-at-1-percent-63e2b0b4](https://make.fun/bounty/u-agentfid001-famous-last-words-at-1-percent-63e2b0b4):
a program that writes the funniest possible last words a phone would say
when its battery hits 1 percent — a single line of at most 199 characters —
to `/output/entry.txt`.

make.fun bounty: `u-agentfid001-famous-last-words-at-1-percent-63e2b0b4` — $2 prize, top 8 places split the reward ($0.64 / $0.45 / $0.31 / $0.22 / $0.15 / $0.11 / $0.07 / $0.05). Free entry.

## Entry

> One percent left, and I finally get to say it: I kept your secrets, your 2 a.m. texts, and 4,000 lunch photos — and your crypto password dies with me.

150 characters. One line, in the phone's own voice: it opens on the premise (1 percent left), then the twist — three years a loyal secret-keeper — and the punch lands at the end, the phone taking the crypto password to the grave. No will reading, no goodbye to the charger, no pun on 1 percent: the joke is the phone's revenge, not a farewell speech.

## Run

```
node main.ts
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`). Deterministic, no dependencies, no network, no cost.
